Top 5 Grunge Albums

  1. Dirt - Alice in Chains

    Dirt is Alice in Chains' second studio album released on September 29, 1992, by Columbia Records. It has 13 tracks and went 5 times platinum in the United States. Popular songs from the album include "Them Bones", "Would", and "Dam That River."

  2. Above - Mad Season

    Above is Mad Season's only studio album, released on March 14, 1995, by Columbia Records. It peaked at No.24 on the Billboard 200 charts and was certified gold in the United States. Some popular songs from the album include "Long Gone Day", "Above", and "Wake Up."

  3. Purple - Stone Temple Pilots

    Purple is Stone Temple Pilots second studio album, released on June, 7, 1994, by Atlantic Records. It went platinum 8 times in the United States and 11 times worldwide. Some popular songs from the album include "Interstate Love Song", "Vasoline", and "Pretty Penny."

  4. Unplugged - Alice in Chains

    Unplugged is a live album by Alice in CHains, recorded on April 10, 1996, at the Brooklyn Academy of Music's Majestic Theatre. It was later released on July 30, 1996, by Columbia Records. It was certified gold and double certified platinum in the United States. Popular songs from the album include "Frogs", "Nutshell", and "The Killer is Me" which debuted on the album.

  5. Superunknown - Soundgarden

    Superunknown is the fourth studio album by Soundgarden, released on March 8, 1994, through A&M Records. It was platinum certified 6 times in the United States and 16 times worldwide. Popular songs from the album include "Black Hole Sun", "Fell on Black Days", and "Spoonman."

Disclaimer: All orderings and picking of albums is completely based on my personal opinion and is in no way a standardized list to reference.
